## Built natively on Bitcoin Cash

guanaco.fi is a non-custodial decentralized exchange built directly on Bitcoin Cash L1. It uses native BCH scripts—no bridges, no wrapping, no L2, and no intermediaries.

- **Custom fee tiers:** Liquidity providers choose their own swap fee percentage.
- **On-chain markets:** Swap BCH and CashTokens through available liquidity pools.
- **Self-custody:** Your keys, your coins.
- **GUA buyback and burn:** A portion of Guanaco pool fees is allocated to periodic open-market GUA buybacks and permanent token burns.

## GUA

GUA is the native fixed-supply CashToken of guanaco.fi on Bitcoin Cash. Its maximum supply is **1,000,000 GUA**, with no further minting.

Learn about the token, its verified identity, supply, and protocol utility at **[guanaco.fi/gua](https://guanaco.fi/gua)**.
